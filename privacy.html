Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Purpose

HEALTHCARE INTELLIGENCE is a personal-use project designed to explore relationships between sleep patterns and subjective health, energy, and recovery.

2. Data Collected

With authorization, the application may access Oura sleep data, including sleep duration, sleep scores, sleep efficiency, awakenings, nighttime heart rate, heart rate variability, and temperature deviations.

The user may also provide voluntary daily health notes and subjective well-being records.

3. Use of Data

Data is used for personal health tracking, longitudinal analysis, and identifying potential patterns. The application is not intended to provide medical diagnoses.

4. Storage and Security

Personal health records are intended to be stored in access-controlled Google Drive and Google Sheets resources. They will not be published in this public GitHub repository.

5. Data Sharing

This project is intended for personal use. Data will not be sold or publicly shared. Authorized technical service providers may process data as necessary to operate the system, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Retention and Deletion

Data may be retained for longitudinal analysis. The user can revoke Oura access and delete stored data under their control. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously stored copies.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the system's functionality and data handling practices are finalized.
